## 1. Why we protect information

Our customers trust us with their goods **and** with the details of the people who receive them. A delayed truck is
recoverable; a leaked consignee list or a stopped warehouse during peak season is not. Information security is
therefore part of how we deliver, not an IT side project.

## 2. Our commitments

1. **Protect** customer, consignee, employee and payment information from unauthorised access, change or loss.
2. **Keep operations running**: the WMS, portal and warehouses must recover within the times we promise our customers.
3. **Meet our obligations**: the UAE PDPL, the information assurance expectations of our government-related clients,
   our contracts and our insurer's conditions (see the [requirements register](interested-parties.md)).
4. **Decide by risk**: we assess risks with one method, treat those above our appetite and accept the rest knowingly.
5. **Improve continually**: we measure, audit, review and fix — and we record what we fix.

## 3. Information security objectives for 2026–2027

| # | Objective | Measure | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O-1 | No personal data breach that must be notified to the regulator | Notifiable breaches | 0 per year | Data Protection Officer |
| O-2 | Keep risk inside appetite | Risks with target score above 9 | 0 | Information Security Manager |
| O-3 | Recover the WMS within the promised time | Successful DR test within RTO 8 h / RPO 1 h | Twice a year | Head of IT |
| O-4 | Build a security-aware workforce | Awareness completion · phishing click rate | ≥ 95% · ≤ 5% | HR Director |
| O-5 | Remove leaver access on time | Leavers disabled by end of last working day | 100% | Head of IT |

Progress is reported each quarter to the ISMS Steering Committee and at each management review
([KPI/KRI table](../05-review/management-review-minutes-2026-10.md)).

## 4. Rules everyone follows

* Use only company-approved devices, accounts and cloud services for company information.
* Never share your login, including scanner and shared-desk logins. Use MFA wherever it is offered.
* Handle information according to its classification label: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.
* Report anything suspicious at once with the *Report phishing* button or to the security mailbox. Reporting a
  mistake is always welcome; hiding one is a disciplinary matter.
* Return all equipment and badges when you leave or change role.

## 5. Topic-specific policies

This policy is supported by topic policies approved by the Information Security Manager: acceptable use, access control,
classification and handling, cryptography, supplier security, incident management, backup and continuity, secure
development, logging and monitoring, and physical security.

## 6. Responsibilities and exceptions

Roles are defined in the [RACI](roles-and-responsibilities.md). Exceptions to any policy must be requested in writing,
assessed as a risk, approved by the risk owner and the Information Security Manager, and given an end date.
Breaches of policy are handled through the disciplinary process (A.6.4).
